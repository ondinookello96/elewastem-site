ElewaSTEM privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ElewaSTEM is an AI tutor for science, maths and computing, written for learners aged about 10 to 14. It runs inside the Anna app platform. It is an early version, and the AI can make mistakes.

What the app asks for

ElewaSTEM does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers or school names. It offers an optional choice of country, which is only a general location and is used to pick local examples. It does not ask for a precise location. Please do not type personal details into the question box.

What is sent to get an answer

When you ask a question, the app sends the following to Anna’s AI service so that an answer can be written:

  • your question and up to the last six question-and-answer pairs of the current conversation;
  • your chosen language, subject, topic and country (if you chose one);
  • your accessibility settings, because they change how answers are written.

Accessibility settings such as dyslexia-friendly text or audio description can hint at a learner’s needs, so turn on only the options you want to use. Anna’s own terms and privacy policy apply to this processing. Anna may use AI models run by other companies, and the developer of ElewaSTEM does not control which one is used.

What the app saves

  • Your chosen language, your accessibility settings and your country choice (if you made one), so the app remembers them next time. These are saved with Anna’s app storage for your account.
  • The conversation itself is not saved between visits. Reloading the app or pressing “New conversation” clears it.

Reporting a wrong answer

If you press “Report a wrong answer”, the app saves the question, the answer (shortened if it is very long), the language, subject and topic, the name of the AI model that answered, and the time. It keeps only the 50 most recent reports. The app does not send reports anywhere else.

Read aloud

The “Read aloud” button uses the speech voices built into your device. Depending on the device, the text may be handled by the device’s operating system or by a cloud voice service run by its maker. If you prefer not to, do not use the button.

Technical messages

If something goes wrong, the app may write an error message to the developer console of your own browser. The app does not send these messages anywhere, but the platform it runs in may collect technical logs.

Children

Learners under 13 should use ElewaSTEM together with a parent, guardian or teacher, and only where Anna’s terms allow it. The app is not designed to collect personal information from anyone.

AI answers can be wrong

ElewaSTEM is a study helper, not a teacher, a doctor or an emergency service. Check important answers with a teacher or a textbook. If you feel upset or unsafe, talk to a trusted adult.

Changes to this notice

If the app changes in a way that affects this notice, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
