ElewaSTEM privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ElewaSTEM is an AI tutor for science, maths and computing, written for learners aged about 10 to 14. It runs inside the Anna app platform. It is an early version, and the AI can make mistakes.

What the app does not ask for

ElewaSTEM does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, home locations or school names. Please do not type any of these into the question box.

What happens to your questions

When you ask a question, the app sends it, along with the recent part of your conversation, to Anna’s AI service so that an answer can be written. Anna’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing. Anna may use AI models run by other companies, and the developer of ElewaSTEM does not control which one is used.

What the app saves

Your chosen language and your accessibility settings (for example large text or high contrast), so the app remembers them next time. These are saved with Anna’s app storage for your account.

The conversation itself is not saved between visits. Pressing “New conversation” clears it straight away.

Reporting a wrong answer

If you press “Report a wrong answer”, the app saves that question and the answer in Anna’s app storage for your account, so the mistake can be checked. It keeps only the most recent reports. The app does not send reports anywhere else.

Children

Learners under 13 should use ElewaSTEM together with a parent, guardian or teacher, and only where Anna’s terms allow it. The app is not designed to collect personal information from anyone.

AI answers can be wrong

ElewaSTEM is a study helper, not a teacher, a doctor or an emergency service. Check important answers with a teacher or a textbook. If you feel upset or unsafe, talk to a trusted adult.

Changes to this notice

If the app changes in a way that affects this notice, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
