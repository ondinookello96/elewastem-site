Support

Help for learners, parents and teachers.

Contact

Questions, problems or accessibility feedback: cosmasokello24@gmail.com.

Messages are read as time allows.

When you write, please say what you were trying to do and what happened instead. Do not include a child’s name, school or other personal details.

If an answer looks wrong

Press “Report a wrong answer” under the answer. The AI tutor can make mistakes in any language, so please check important answers with a teacher or a textbook.

Languages

English and Kiswahili are the main languages. Other languages are in beta, which means the tutor’s answers may contain mistakes or unusual wording. Native speakers who would like to help check answers are welcome to get in touch.

Accessibility

ElewaSTEM has options for audio description, text-based visual cues, simpler words, step-by-step pacing, dyslexia-friendly text, high contrast and large text. It is designed to work with screen readers and a keyboard, but it is still being tested with learners who use them. Feedback from screen-reader users, teachers and parents is especially helpful.

The “Read aloud” button appears only when your device has a voice for the chosen language, so it may be missing for some languages.

Starting a new topic

Press “New conversation” to clear the current conversation. Your language and accessibility settings stay as they are.
